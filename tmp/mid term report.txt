Chapter 1 



Introduction to HelpSetu
















* Inspiration and Ideation: How the Project Idea Emerged

The idea for HelpSetu emerged from our collective recognition of the challenges faced by our local community, both in rural and urban areas. As community members ourselves, we were deeply concerned about issues such as domestic violence, child labour, inadequate child education, and illegal logging, which often went unreported or unnoticed due to reluctance to engage in formal procedures. We realized the need for a centralized platform where individuals could easily report such concerns and receive timely assistance without fear of repercussion.




* Objectives: Aim of  This Portal


Our aim is to establish a centralized and user-friendly portal where locals can easily report any issues within their locality without engaging in formal procedures and without compromising their privacy and security. Subsequently, we will ensure that reported issues are promptly forwarded to the relevant authorities of that area for necessary action.
